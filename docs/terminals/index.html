Recovered terminals

The ends of the old outline bands, cut at their seams, before anything is redrawn.

Each cell is one end of one band: join on the left, tip in the middle, join back on the right. The percentage is the measured taper fraction — how much of the band the taper occupies — not the value the source page claims.
