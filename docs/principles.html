Earth

primal

Rises in strata, each shelf flatter than the one below. Weight sits at the foot and leaves through edges that cut, not through curves. The eye should read stacked horizontal pressure, not a shape.

proportion1 : 1.6 (width : height)
width to length1 : 8
max turn per unit35° at a corner, 0° along a stratum

Not this: a mountain outline, a leaf, a zigzag

Water

primal

A long sweep that folds back once and settles. Width is nearly constant; the gesture lives in the direction, not the thickness. A single drop may swell the line, but only one.

proportion1 : 3 (height : length)
width to length1 : 12
turn per sweep≤ 90°

Not this: a wave icon, three identical waves, a splash

Air (cloud)

primal

Billows of falling radius, each one leaning the same way, closed by round ends. Nothing sharp: the silhouette is a sequence of domes, and the last is the smallest.

proportion1 : 1.2
width to length1 : 6
billow turn120° – 180° per billow

Not this: a cloud icon, a scalloped border, smoke

Fire

primal

Rises fast, then falls to a point. Curvature climbs to a maximum and is released; one corner is allowed, a smooth return is not. The tip is the design, not the base.

proportion1 : 2.5
width to length1 : 10
tip turn≤ 25° at the point

Not this: a candle flame, a leaf, a tear drop

Fleur-de-lis

archetype

A central lance between two petals that bow outward and return, bound by a band and closed by a foot. Symmetry is exact; the band is the only horizontal.

proportion1 : 2.2
width to length1 : 7
band height≤ 8% of total height

Not this: a lily, a crown, a spearhead

Acanthus

archetype

A stem of lobes that alternate and overlap, each lobe curling under the next. Depth comes from overlap, not shading; the leaf narrows toward the tip and widens at the base.

proportion1 : 1.4
width to length1 : 5
lobe turn60° – 90° per lobe

Not this: a fern, a maple leaf, a scroll

Palmette

archetype

A fan of leaves rising from one point, the centre leaf tallest and each neighbour shorter. The spacing is regular; the outer leaves bend away hardest.

proportion1 : 1.8
width to length1 : 9
leaf count5 – 9 leaves, 12° – 20° apart

Not this: a sunburst, a peacock tail, a tree

Scroll (volute)

archetype

A spiral that tightens without closing and leaves on its own tangent. The eye is a counter, not an ink shape; the line never crosses itself.

proportion1 : 1
width to length1 : 8
spiral turn540° total, radius halving each half-turn

Not this: a spiral icon, a snail shell, a hook
