Primal ornaments

Earth, water, air, fire — one grammar each, no fills.

earth

Strata: four terraces, real corners, heavy at the foot, drawn to a taper.

water

Flow: a long sweep, a reversal, a shorter return; both ends rounded.

air

Cloud: billows of falling radius, round ends, nothing sharp.

fire

Tongue: curvature rising then falling, a corner in the middle, a point at the tip.
