## Reference images — `reference/`

Curvature, width, direction and symmetry were measured from published images;
no shape is traced. Every tracked file is attributed in
`reference/ATTRIBUTION.md`, with its source URL, licence and author. Each
remains under the licence recorded there (mostly CC BY-SA 2.0 / 4.0 and
public domain), not under this project's MIT licence.

Photographs that are not in `ATTRIBUTION.md` are not part of the repository.
